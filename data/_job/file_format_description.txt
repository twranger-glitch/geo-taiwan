======================================================================
1. RAW TIME SERIES FILES (*.series)
======================================================================

The raw time series files (*.series) follow the GipsyX StationSeries format and contain daily GNSS position solutions after basic quality control and outlier screening.

Column format:

1. decimal_year
2. east
3. north
4. up
5. sigma_east
6. sigma_north
7. sigma_up
8. corr_EN
9. corr_EV
10. corr_NV
11. jsec
12. year
13. month
14. day
15. hour
16. minute
17. second

Column descriptions:

decimal_year
    Observation epoch expressed as decimal year.

east
    East component of the station position time series.

north
    North component of the station position time series.

up
    Up component of the station position time series.

sigma_east
    Formal uncertainty of the east component.

sigma_north
    Formal uncertainty of the north component.

sigma_up
    Formal uncertainty of the up component.

corr_EN
    Correlation coefficient between east and north components.

corr_EV
    Correlation coefficient between east and vertical components.

corr_NV
    Correlation coefficient between north and vertical components.

jsec
    Seconds past 2000-01-01 11:59:47 UTC following the GCORE convention.

year, month, day, hour, minute, second
    Calendar date and time corresponding to the observation epoch.

Units:

decimal_year      year
east              meter
north             meter
up                meter
sigma_east        meter
sigma_north       meter
sigma_up          meter
corr_EN           dimensionless
corr_EV           dimensionless
corr_NV           dimensionless
jsec              second

Reference ellipsoid:

GRS80

Example:

2000.38359856 0.004366 0.013474 0.007587 0.001635 0.001492 0.008474 0.023321 2.483273 0.394676 12105450.00 2000 5 20 14 37 30

======================================================================
2. CORRECTED TIME SERIES FILES (*.enu)
======================================================================

The corrected time series files are provided for:
(1) offset-corrected time series
(2) seasonal-filtered time series

These files are expressed in local east-north-up (ENU) coordinates relative to the reference position adopted in the processing.

Column format:

1. decimal_year
2. date
3. east
4. north
5. up
6. sigma_north
7. sigma_east
8. sigma_up

Column descriptions:

decimal_year
    Observation epoch expressed as decimal year.

date
    Observation date in YYYY-MM-DD format.

east
    East component of the station displacement time series.

north
    North component of the station displacement time series.

up
    Up component of the station displacement time series.

sigma_north
    Formal uncertainty of the north component.

sigma_east
    Formal uncertainty of the east component.

sigma_up
    Formal uncertainty of the up component.

Units:

decimal_year      year
east              mm
north             mm
up                mm
sigma_north       mm
sigma_east        mm
sigma_up          mm

Example:

2003.00274 2003-01-01 -16.3440 41.3540 11.7950 1.2030 1.4910 7.1360

Notes:

1. The *.series files represent the original processed PPP daily solutions after basic quality control.
2. The *.enu files represent processed displacement time series after applying additional corrections.
3. Offset-corrected files remove documented instrumental and coseismic offsets.
4. Seasonal-filtered files further remove annual and semi-annual seasonal signals derived from time-series modeling.
5. Missing or rejected daily solutions are not included in the files.
6. File names follow the station code convention, for example:
       S001.series
       S001.enu
7. The ENU coordinate system follows the local topocentric reference frame convention:
       E = east
       N = north
       U = up
